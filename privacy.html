Mathlight Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 23, 2026

Support Privacy Policy Impressum

In short

  • Mathlight does not collect your data. The developer never sees what you do in the app.
  • Your progress stays on your iPhone.
  • No account, no ads, no analytics, no tracking.
  • Purchases go through Apple. The developer never gets your payment details.

What Mathlight stores

The lessons you finished, your practice answers (to show your streak, daily goal and skills) and a few settings, such as the last course you opened. All of it is stored only on your iPhone. Mathlight has no server and sends nothing over the internet.

Mathlight Plus

Mathlight Plus is a subscription through the App Store. Apple handles the payment. The app only learns whether Plus is active for your Apple Account, and the developer never gets your name, email or payment details.

What Mathlight doesn’t do

  • No analytics, crash trackers or advertising.
  • No tracking across other apps or websites.
  • No selling or sharing of data, because none is collected.

Deleting your data

Deleting the app removes everything it stored on your iPhone.

This website

This site is hosted by GitHub Pages. It uses no cookies, no analytics and no fonts or scripts from other servers. GitHub may log your IP address for security, as described in GitHub’s privacy statement.

Emails and your rights

If you write to the developer, your email address and message are used only to answer you (Art. 6(1)(b) and (f) GDPR) and are deleted once they are no longer needed, unless the law requires keeping them longer. You have the right to access, correct and delete your data, to restrict or object to its use, and to data portability (Art. 15–21 GDPR). You can also complain to a data protection authority, for example the one in North Rhine-Westphalia (LDI NRW).

Children

Mathlight doesn’t collect personal data from anyone, children included.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
