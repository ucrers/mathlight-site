Need help?

Write to ucar.ersin@outlook.de. Tell me your iPhone model, your iOS version, the lesson and what happened. I usually answer within a few days.

Questions

  • Which language does the app use? The language of your iPhone: English, German or Turkish. To change it for Mathlight only, open the Settings app → Apps → Mathlight → Language.
  • Why is a lesson locked? Unit 1 of every course is free. The other units need Mathlight Plus.
  • How many practice problems are free? 5 right answers a day. Plus has no limit.
  • Where is my progress saved? Only on your iPhone. Deleting the app deletes your progress.

Mathlight Plus

  • Bought Plus before, or got a new iPhone? Open the paywall and tap Restore Purchases, with the same Apple Account.
  • Cancel in the Settings app → your name → Subscriptions → Mathlight, at least 24 hours before it renews.
  • Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.
